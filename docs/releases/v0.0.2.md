# v0.0.2

The second pre-release tag of recordprims: the complete pure-Go
`durable-record/v1` store (`recordprims-fs/v2`), passing all 400 vectors of the
pinned `3leaps/crucible` `v0.2.10` contract. The Rust twin and language
bindings land before 0.1.0.

## What it ships

- **Go module** `github.com/3leaps/recordprims`, cgo-free, on Go 1.26 or
  later. The Rust crate and the TypeScript and Python bindings are not yet
  published in this release.
- **Contract conformance (`durable-record/v1`).** Pinned to `3leaps/crucible`
  `v0.2.10` (`24c8920`), with strict wire ingest (safe-integer domain,
  duplicate-key and lone-surrogate refusal), bounded Draft 2020-12 schema
  validation (`strict-rfc` and `structural-leap/v1`), canonical v1 instant
  parsing (`ParseInstant`), and all 400 `durable-record/v1` conformance
  vectors passing.
- **Streams and journal (`recordprims-fs/v2`).** `OpenStream` (`WithContinues`),
  `Append`, `Retire`, `Protect`, `Reconcile`, `Head`, `Revisions`, and
  `Release`:
  - Keyed idempotent writes and retirements within stream-scoped windows (`live`
    and `remember`), typed `Receipt` and `Reconciliation` envelopes, and
    bounded out-of-space partial-write abort.
  - Protection (`tombstoned` and `untombstoned` holds), continuations with
    `imported_from`, `restrictions_reviewed`, detached stream seals and
    ancestor closure.
  - Callback-scoped `Release` (`Frame`) that revokes the body reader on return
    and refuses callback reentry (`ErrSinkReentered`).
  - Designated `body_removal_stream` commit points for `PutBody` and
    `RemoveBody`, removal eligibility (`P1`–`P5`), and designated-stream
    recovery (`R1`–`R19`).
  - Watermarked local stream indexes (`heads.idx`, `keys.idx`, `records.idx`)
    rebuilt deterministically from the journal when absent or behind.
- **Internal bucket CAS stratum (`internal/stratum/bucketcas`).** Segmented
  NDJSON journal groups, `head.json` conditional-PUT linearization, journal
  checkpoints (bounding cold-start object reads while retaining full journal
  history), and indeterminate-outcome recovery.
- **Repository and license updates.** ADR-0001 records the Profile B twin and
  binding plan; `LICENSE-APACHE` and `LICENSE-MIT` carry the verbatim license
  texts with project notices in `NOTICE`.

## Platforms

CI runs `make check` on Linux amd64/arm64 inside the digest-pinned
`goneat-tools-runner-glibc` `v0.5.15` container (`goneat v0.6.8`), runs
`make check-platform` natively on darwin arm64 and windows amd64/arm64, and
builds and tests the Go module standalone outside the repository.
